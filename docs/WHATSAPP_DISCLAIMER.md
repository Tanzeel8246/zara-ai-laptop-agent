# WhatsApp Disclaimer — please read before enabling WhatsApp

## What this route is

CodeHubb Assistant's WhatsApp transport ("Route A") connects through
**Baileys**, an open-source library that speaks the WhatsApp Web
protocol. You pair it by scanning a QR code with WhatsApp's
**Linked devices** feature, the same way you would link WhatsApp Web.

## What it is not

- It is **not** the official WhatsApp Business / Cloud API.
- It is **not** affiliated with, endorsed by, or supported by WhatsApp
  or Meta.
- Meta does not offer a public API for personal WhatsApp accounts;
  unofficial bridges operate in a grey area of WhatsApp's Terms of
  Service.

## The risk, plainly

**WhatsApp can restrict, log out, or permanently ban a number that uses
an unofficial bridge.** This can happen without warning, and neither
CodeHubb Assistant nor its seller can prevent it, undo it, or compensate
you for it.

## Our strong advice

- **Use a dedicated, secondary number** for the assistant — a cheap
  prepaid SIM you can afford to lose. **Never** link your main personal
  number, and **never** your business number.
- Use the linked account to talk to the assistant, not for bulk
  messaging, marketing, or messaging people who did not ask to be
  contacted — that behaviour attracts bans fastest.
- Keep message volume human-like. The assistant is a personal tool, not
  a broadcasting platform.

## If your number gets restricted

Re-pairing with the same number usually will not help while a
restriction is active. Pair a different dedicated number instead (setup
wizard → Chats & messaging → WhatsApp), and review the advice above.

## The future official route

An **official WhatsApp Cloud API route** (Meta's supported API, via a
relay operated by the seller) is planned as a future **Pro** feature.
It removes the ban risk of the unofficial bridge; until it ships, Route
A above is what v1.0 offers, and enabling it means you accept this
disclaimer (also referenced in the EULA, §5).
