Questions

The honest answers, up front.

What does "BYOK" mean — do I pay twice?

Bring Your Own Key: you use your own AI account — a Claude subscription you already have, or an API key from OpenAI, Gemini, DeepSeek and others — so AI usage is billed to you by that provider, at their prices, with no markup from us. The license pays only for the software. With a Claude subscription, there is no per-token AI cost at all.

Is the WhatsApp connection official?

No — in v1.0 it is an unofficial bridge (the same way WhatsApp Web links a browser), and WhatsApp can restrict numbers that use such bridges. We say this plainly everywhere: use a dedicated secondary number, never your main or business number. An official Cloud API route is planned as a future Pro feature. Telegram and Discord use their official APIs.

Which Windows versions are supported?

Windows 10 and 11. v1.0 is Windows-only — there is no Mac or Linux version.

Does my PC need to stay on?

Yes — the assistant lives on your PC, so it must be on, logged in and unlocked for screen and mouse work. Instructions sent while it sleeps are queued and run when it wakes; a missed scheduled job fires once on return, never in a burst.

Can I name my assistant?

Yes — the persona name is yours to choose during setup ("Zara" is just the default). It answers to whatever name you give it.

What happens when my trial or subscription ends?

The assistant stops acting on instructions, but nothing is deleted — your settings, memory and files stay on your PC, and everything resumes when you activate a valid key.
